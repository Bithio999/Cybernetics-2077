# Tetratronic Rippler

[All cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Installation](installation.md) · [Daily stock](ripperdocs.md#daily-stock)

<img src="assets/icons/tetratronic_rippler.png" alt="Tetratronic Rippler" width="48" height="48" class="item-icon">

Combat quickhacks followed immediately by a queued Control quickhack on the **same target** deal **40% more damage**. Weapon attacks deal **15% more damage** to targets affected by a Control quickhack. Base damage bonus is **5% per tier above 1**.
