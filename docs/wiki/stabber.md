| System | Tiers | Capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Nervous System | 2–5 | 12 |

Adds critical chance to **swords, Mantis Blades and thrown tridents**. Weapon criticals deal **1.5×** damage before Berserk bonuses. Independent cyberware crit chances combine as probabilities, capped at **50%** total; they do not stack into multiple critical multipliers. Normal Minecraft jump-critical hits still work.

| Tier | Added critical chance |
| --- | ---: |
| 2 | 8% |
| 3 | 12% |
| 4 | 16% |
| 5 | 20% |
