# Cripple Movement

[Wiki home](index.md) · [Cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Quickhacks](quickhacks.md) · [Village services](ripperdocs.md)

<img src="assets/icons/cripple_movement.png" alt="Cripple Movement icon" width="48" height="48" class="item-icon">

**Tiers:** 2–5 · **Damage type:** None

Suppresses movement, interrupts jumping and disables player flight. It deals no direct damage.

| Tier | RAM | Duration |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 2 | 2 | 4 s |
| 3 | 3 | 5 s |
| 4 | 4 | 6 s |
| 5 | 5 | 7 s |

Raven relays apply half the normal control duration.
