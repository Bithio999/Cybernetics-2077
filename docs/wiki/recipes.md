## Smithing upgrades

Use a **vanilla smithing table** after removing the implant or chip. Put **redstone dust in the template slot**, the previous-tier item in the middle, and the material below on the right.

| Upgrade | Right-slot material |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 → 2 | Gold block |
| Tier 2 → 3 | Diamond |
| Tier 3 → 4 | Netherite ingot |
| Tier 4 → 5 | Nether star |

This applies to Basic Kiroshi Optics, Militech Paraline and Short Circuit. Mantis Blades upgrade from **tier 2 onward**; they have no tier-1 craft. Loaded programs and custom item data carry over. New upgrade recipes unlock after obtaining the preceding tier.
