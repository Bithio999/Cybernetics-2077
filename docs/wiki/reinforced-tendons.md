| Legs | 2–5 | 8 |

Press **jump again in midair** for one extra jump. It resets on landing, swimming or climbing. Flying and riding disable it. Normal fall damage applies.

| Tier | Second-jump upward impulse |
| --- | ---: |
| 2 | 0.49 |
| 3 | 0.525 |
| 4 | 0.56 |
| 5 | 0.595 |

Only one leg implant can be installed.
