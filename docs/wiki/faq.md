# Common questions

[Wiki home](index.md) · [Cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Quickhacks](quickhacks.md) · [Village services](ripperdocs.md)

## Does it work in an existing world?

Yes. Existing equipment and player data remain compatible. New village buildings require newly generated chunks; existing ripperdocs receive the current shop behaviour.

## Why does the scanner not open?

Install optics in **Face** at a ripperdoc or bench. Carrying the item is not enough. Hold **V**, rather than tapping it. An active contract grants navigation only.

## Why are there no quickhacks in the scanner?

Install a cyberdeck in **Operating System** and load chips through **Manage Quickhacks**. A chip cannot exceed the deck's tier. Berserk occupies that same socket and replaces a cyberdeck.

## Why can I not install an implant?

Check its body system, your remaining capacity, duplicate-family restrictions and whether that system has a free socket. The inventory cyberware viewer is read-only; use a ripperdoc's Install tab or a bench.

## Why is an item missing from the shop?

Stock rotates daily and is filtered by Street Cred. Another doctor may carry it, or it may appear after dawn. Newly unlocked tiers begin uncommon. Kiroshi Optics tier 1 are always stocked at Street Cred 0–10.

## How do I get more Street Cred?

The current **First Impressions** contract grants **5 once**. More jobs have not been added yet. See [Street Cred](street-cred.md#street-cred) for current progression and thresholds.

## My fixer has no clinic route

Visit a clinic in the same dimension, then return. A lost active contract can be reissued by either fixer.

## Why will Mantis Blades not deploy?

Both hands must be empty when pressing **G**. Once deployed, changing your selected item keeps them active, but held items remain inactive until you retract.

## Where is the download?

Start at [Getting started](getting-started.md). Use the same mod version on server and clients.
