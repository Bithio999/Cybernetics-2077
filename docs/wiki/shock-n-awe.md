| Integumentary System | 1–5 | 25 |

After taking damage from an enemy, may release an **electrical** discharge within **4 blocks**, targeting the attacker and nearby enemies with clear line of sight. Maximum **8 targets**, **2-second internal cooldown**. Retaliation cannot trigger another Shock-n-Awe discharge.

**Damage type:** Electrical; bypasses armor, respects other applicable damage defenses and team/PvP rules. Also grants armor and toughness.
