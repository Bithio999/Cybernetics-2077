# Creative Sandevistan

[Cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Time bubble](dynalar-sandevistan.md#time-bubble)

<img src="assets/icons/creative_sandevistan.png" alt="Creative Sandevistan" width="48" height="48" class="item-icon">

A **Tier 5 Operating System**, available only from the **Cybernetics 2077 Creative tab** or commands. It does not appear in ripperdoc stock, mob loadouts, drops or crafting recipes.

| Effect | Value |
| --- | ---: |
| Slow relative to the user | 99.9% |
| Radius | 32 blocks |
| Capacity | 0 |
| Full battery | 10 seconds |
| Full recharge while inactive | 30 seconds |
| Rearm after deactivation | 1 second |

Install it in the Operating System socket through a cybernetics bench or a ripperdoc's **Install** tab. Press **O** to activate or deactivate. Early deactivation saves remaining charge; activation needs at least 20% charge. It grants no additional critical-hit bonuses.

Your pace is **125% of normal**. Ordinary affected entities and supported environment updates run at **0.125% of normal**, or **0.1% of your pace**. Equal models retain equal speed; overlapping fields use the strongest effect. PvP and ally protection still apply. The sun and shared weather clock retain normal speed.

Supported TaCZ bullets and firing mechanisms follow the slowed world clock; reloading stays normal. Removing the implant, dying, logging out or changing dimension ends activation. You can continue using an installed copy after switching out of Creative mode.

Command: `/give @s cybernetics2077:creative_sandevistan_tier_5`
