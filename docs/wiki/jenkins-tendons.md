| Legs | 2–5 | 6 |

Boosts sprinting speed, tapering from the fresh bonus to the sustained bonus over **5 seconds** of sprinting. Restores freshness while not sprinting at the same rate (up to **5 seconds** to fully recover). Walking and crouching are unaffected. Removing/reinstalling does not reset fatigue.

| Tier | Fresh sprint speed | Sustained sprint speed |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | +24% | +4% |
| 3 | +36% | +6% |
| 4 | +48% | +8% |
| 5 | +60% | +10% |
