| System | Tiers | Capacity |

| Frontal Cortex | 3–5 | 10 |

Increases maximum RAM and speeds up quickhack uploads. Requires an installed cyberdeck.

| Tier | Added RAM | Upload speed bonus |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 3 | 3 | +15% |
| 4 | 4 | +20% |
| 5 | 5 | +25% |

Upload ticks are divided by the speed multiplier and rounded to a whole tick. Uploads cannot be shorter than 6 ticks. RAM stays capped to your equipped loadout.
