# Reboot Optics

[Wiki home](index.md) · [Cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Quickhacks](quickhacks.md) · [Village services](ripperdocs.md)

<img src="assets/icons/reboot_optics.png" alt="Reboot Optics icon" width="48" height="48" class="item-icon">

**Tiers:** 1–5 · **Damage type:** None

Blinds players and clears/blocks mob attack targeting. It deals no direct damage.

| Tier | RAM | Duration |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| 1 | 2 | 4 s |
| 2 | 2 | 5 s |
| 3 | 3 | 6 s |
| 4 | 3 | 7 s |
| 5 | 4 | 8 s |

Raven relays apply half the normal control duration.
