| Skeleton | 3–5 | 25 |

Multiplies total armor, including equipment and other implants. Adds flat armor toughness. Both use Minecraft’s normal armor calculations; armor-bypassing damage ignores them.

| Tier | Total armor | Added toughness |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | +12% | 0.9 |
| 4 | +16% | 1.2 |
| 5 | +20% | 1.5 |
