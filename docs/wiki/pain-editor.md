# Pain Editor

[Wiki home](index.md) · [Cyberware](cyberware.md) · [Quickhacks](quickhacks.md) · [Village services](ripperdocs.md)

<img src="assets/icons/pain_editor.png" alt="Pain Editor icon" width="48" height="48" class="item-icon">

| System | Tiers | Capacity |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Integumentary System | 4–5 | 35 |

Reduces incoming damage after normal defenses, including damaging quickhacks. Also provides armor and toughness. Does not stop control hacks or bypassing-invulnerability damage such as the void and `/kill`.

| Tier | Added armor | Added toughness | Damage reduction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 4 | 4 | 1.6 | 8% |
| 5 | 5 | 2.0 | 10% |
