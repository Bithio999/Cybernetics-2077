# Raven Microcyber and quickhacks

Reviewed 18 November 2026. These current 2.x game database entries provided the starting tiers and core functions:

- [Raven Microcyber](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/14474/raven-microcyber): starts at tier 3; emphasizes quickhack spreading, with doubled spread distance; tier-5 reference has 9 RAM, 8 sockets and 20 capacity.
- [Overheat](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/1048/overheat): starts at tier 1, thermal damage over time.
- [Contagion](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/1029/contagion): starts at tier 2, chemical damage and spreading; tier 4 enables ignition detonation.
- [Reboot Optics](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/1025/reboot-optics): starts at tier 1, interferes with detection and vision.
- [Cripple Movement](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/15084/cripple-movement): starts at tier 2, disables movement and movement cyberware.
- [Synapse Burnout](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/15090/synapse-burnout): starts at tier 3, physical damage increasing with spent RAM.

Minecraft adaptation: health, RAM costs, durations and tier growth use the existing mod's scale. Raven has deterministic relays for all supported programs, allowing its spreading playstyle to work with the available loadout. Control uses blindness, target interruption and movement suppression. The initial implementation covers the core effects and Contagion ignition combination; Cyberpunk's perk-specific Overclock and finisher branches are outside this release. Item/chip sprites are original 32px artwork.
