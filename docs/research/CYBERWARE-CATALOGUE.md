# Cyberpunk 2077 cyberware catalogue

Research reference for the current 2.x game, including Phantom Liberty, reviewed 17 November 2026. Entries are grouped as in the [Gamestegy cyberware database](https://gamestegy.com/cyberpunk-2077/wiki/cyberware). Each name links to its database entry. This is a reference list, not a list of implants implemented in this mod. Tier and “+” upgrades do not count as separate implant models.

Our existing items map to **Basic Kiroshi Optics**, **Militech Paraline** and **Mantis Blades**. **Short Circuit** is a quickhack program, not body cyberware. The mod implements those three families plus [14 more implants](../IMPLANTS.md). Mantis Blades start at tier 2; every family continues through tier 5 from its researched starting tier. [Minecraft effects and upgrade recipes](../CYBERWARE.md).

## Source boundaries

The current sections above contain **121 entries**. They include elemental arm variants, Iconic cyberware and quest tattoos. Cut content and tabletop-only implants are outside this list. The legacy appendix follows the reviewed database rather than claiming every historical patch variant.

The catalogue page was accessible. Its individual item pages returned 403 during this review, and Fandom returned 402; no inaccessible page was treated as freshly reviewed. The catalogue itself supplies the names, systems, tier ranges and effect summaries used here.

[CD PROJEKT RED Update 2.0 notes](https://www.cyberpunk.net/en/news/49060/update-2-0) confirm cyberware capacity, ripperdoc upgrading and the switch to tiers. Minecraft ranges, costs and damage in this mod are adaptation values, not Cyberpunk damage conversions.
